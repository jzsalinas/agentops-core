# Governance

AgentOps is an independent project governed by its maintainer. The maintainer
controls repository administration, review assignments, acceptance of changes,
release approval, and appointment or removal of additional maintainers.

Issues, Pull Requests, prior collaboration, or use of AgentOps do not confer
ownership of the project, administrative access, approval rights, or authority
over another consumer. Rights granted by the MIT license are distinct from
repository administrative roles.

## Changes and approval

Anyone may propose a change with motivation, compatibility impact, security effects,
and reproducible tests. The maintainer decides whether to accept, request changes,
defer, or reject it. An agent may assist with analysis but cannot impersonate a
human reviewer or grant itself maintainer authority.

Normative contracts, policy minima, instructions, validators, permissions, and
release tooling require explicit maintainer approval. Seek an independent review
for security-sensitive changes. If none is available, record that limit and defer
claims requiring independent review; do not invent a reviewer. CI success is
validation evidence, not authorization to merge or publish.

Editorial-only changes require review proportionate to their scope. Record material
architectural decisions and migration requirements. Release approval identifies
exact content digests and unresolved limitations.

## Consumers

A consumer's owners retain authority over its identities, resources, budgets,
credentials, tools, and acceptance policy. The maintainer cannot enlarge that
authority through a standard release, comment, or public instruction update.
Adoption is explicit and version-bound. Third-party contributions are proposals
until accepted; consumer updates are proposals until locally adopted.
