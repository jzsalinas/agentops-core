# Proportional adoption checklist

These are assessment outcomes, not new AgentOps wire contracts or certifications.

| Claim | Minimum evidence | Insufficient evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluated | Read-only observations, limits, alternatives and owner response | A repository link alone |
| Documentary guidance adopted | Authenticated publication, fixed version/commit/digest, approved exact diff, existing policy references, adoption record and readback | Unresolved template, copied branch head or an assessment approval |
| Coordinated documentary use | Above plus demonstrated scope, ownership, evidence/review rules and justified delegation using existing controls | Team count or several named roles |
| Operational profile implemented/activated | Separate implementation/version, owner authorization and applicable conformance evidence including failure modes | Documents, CLI output, fixtures or a skill |

Before documentary adoption:

- Check existing equivalents and incompatible policies; preserve them or defer.
- Select only useful guidance; do not require multiagent work for a solo project.
- Verify publication provenance and artifact integrity through a pre-adopted root
  of trust. Record channel, version, commit, digest and verification limits.
- Obtain separate explicit acceptance for a published prerelease. Unpublished
  content remains evaluation-only.
- Resolve every placeholder and preserve root/nested instruction precedence.
- Keep the existing policy and decision mechanism; adoption metadata grants nothing.
- Review the exact patch and owner approval, then inspect the applied diff.
- Distinguish no execution during diagnosis from later owner-approved validation.
- Record no new credentials, infrastructure, external effects or activation.
- Treat update discovery and host skill installation as optional separate selections.
- Preserve the MIT license and attribution with any redistributed material.

Rejecting or deferring adoption passes the assessment when evidence or policy does
not support it. Do not report operational conformance from this checklist.
